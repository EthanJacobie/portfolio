PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

My
Reflection.

During this module, I have learned that being an IT professional involves more than simply writing code. I have developed a better understanding of how technical skills, communication, presentation and professional identity work together. Building this portfolio has also shown me how important it is to present my skills and projects clearly so that another person can quickly understand my abilities.

The IT professional skill I currently consider one of my strongest is problem solving. When working on programming tasks, I am willing to test different solutions, identify errors and make changes until I understand what is causing the problem. My Java coursework has given me practical experience with classes, methods, user input, validation, collections and program structure.

A skill I would like to improve is my web development ability. I want to become more confident with HTML, CSS and JavaScript and understand how to create websites that are both visually professional and technically effective. I also want to improve my understanding of responsive design and accessibility.

To develop this skill, I will continue building small websites and practising by creating projects outside of my normal coursework. I will use documentation, tutorials and feedback to improve my code, and I will regularly review older projects to see how they could be made cleaner or more efficient.

This portfolio can support my future studies and career by becoming a record of my progress. As I complete more university projects, learn additional technologies and gain experience, I can update the website with new work and skills. In the future, it can give potential employers a clear overview of my interests, development and practical experience.
